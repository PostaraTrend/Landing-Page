Privacy Policy

PostaraTrend Technologies Inc. · Effective 6 May 2026 · Last updated 3 October 2026

PostaraTrend Technologies Inc. ("PostaraTrend," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain and protect personal information across our websites and products, including TraceScout and Autopilot Social.

1. Who we are

PostaraTrend Technologies Inc. is an Alberta technology company with its registered office in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. We build and operate products for governed automation, market and opportunity intelligence, and evidence-backed career and hiring workflows.

For personal information we collect directly for our own purposes, PostaraTrend generally acts as the organization responsible for that information. When an employer, recruiting agency or other organization uses a PostaraTrend workspace to process candidate or client information under its own instructions, that organization may be the primary organization responsible for the information and PostaraTrend may act as its service provider or processor.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to personal information collected through:

  • postaratrend.ca, TraceScout websites and applications, and other PostaraTrend-operated websites;
  • TraceScout job-seeker, hiring-team and recruiting-agency workspaces;
  • Autopilot Social and related publishing, intelligence and automation services;
  • digital products, forms, support channels, waitlists, demonstrations and business communications; and
  • connected services or integrations that you authorize us to use.

Third-party websites and services have their own privacy practices. Their policies apply when you use them directly.

3. Personal information we collect

3.1 Identity, contact and account information

  • name, email address, phone number, organization, role and country or region;
  • account credentials and authentication information;
  • communications, support requests, feedback and form submissions; and
  • billing, transaction and subscription records. Full payment-card numbers are handled by payment processors rather than stored by us.

3.2 TraceScout career and job-seeker information

When you use TraceScout as a job seeker, we may process information such as:

  • resumes, CVs, LinkedIn exports and other career documents you provide;
  • employment history, education, qualifications, certifications, skills, achievements and job preferences;
  • your attestations or confirmations about whether resume information is accurate;
  • source-line, provenance, verification and refusal records used to show what evidence supports a generated or tailored statement;
  • job matches, role preferences, saved roles, application status, interview status, follow-up tasks and other application-workflow information;
  • tailored resumes, cover letters, interview-preparation material, practice answers or transcripts, work plans and related generated content; and
  • verification-link activity and reference confirmations where those features are used.

3.3 Employer and recruiting-agency workspace information

Hiring teams and agencies may provide or create job descriptions, company and client information, candidate records, invitations, shortlists, notes, feedback, workflow status, interview information, collaboration records and verification or matching data. Workspace administrators control who in their organization can access that workspace.

3.4 Reference and verification information

If a user asks another person to confirm a specific career statement, we may process the reference person's name, contact details, relationship to the user, confirmation response, timestamps and limited information needed to identify the statement being confirmed. We do not require a reference to see the user's entire resume when the feature is designed to request confirmation of a specific statement.

3.5 Connected publishing and social-platform information

For Autopilot Social or related publishing services, we may process profile information, page or channel identifiers, content, authorized publishing permissions, access tokens and engagement metrics from platforms you connect. Access is limited to permissions you authorize through the relevant platform.

3.6 Technical, usage and public-source information

  • IP address, browser, device, operating system, timestamps, referring pages and product usage events;
  • security, audit, workflow and error logs; and
  • publicly available business, market, job-posting, news or web information used to provide search, matching, intelligence or trend features.

4. How we use personal information

We use personal information to:

  • create, secure and administer accounts and workspaces;
  • read and structure resumes and other submitted content;
  • calculate job-match or candidate-fit information and explain supporting evidence and gaps;
  • generate or tailor application materials, interview-preparation content and work plans;
  • maintain provenance, verification and refusal records intended to reduce unsupported claims;
  • support application tracking, recruiting, hiring and agency workflows;
  • operate authorized publishing, content and market-intelligence workflows;
  • process payments and administer subscriptions or one-time purchases;
  • provide support, service messages and, where permitted, marketing communications;
  • monitor reliability, prevent abuse, investigate security issues and improve our products; and
  • comply with law, enforce agreements and protect users, PostaraTrend and others.
Decision support, not an employment decision. TraceScout match scores, rankings, summaries and AI-generated materials are tools to assist people. They are not guarantees of suitability, employment, promotion or hiring outcomes. Employers and agencies remain responsible for their own hiring decisions and legal obligations.

5. AI-assisted processing

Some PostaraTrend features use artificial intelligence to analyze, classify, summarize, match, generate or verify content. Depending on the feature, information you submit may be transmitted to contracted AI or infrastructure providers to perform that function. We seek to limit the information shared to what is reasonably necessary for the requested service and apply technical and contractual safeguards appropriate to the service.

TraceScout is designed to preserve a distinction between source information supplied or attested by a user and AI-generated or transformed content. Where the product records provenance, refusals or verification results, those records form part of the relevant workspace data.

6. How we disclose personal information

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose it to:

  • service providers that support hosting, authentication, databases, AI processing, payments, email, analytics, customer support, monitoring, rendering, workflow automation or security;
  • employers, agencies, clients or invited collaborators when you use a feature that intentionally shares information with them or when they administer the workspace in which the information is held;
  • references or verification recipients when you request a confirmation or share a verification link;
  • social or publishing platforms when you authorize publishing or connected-account functions;
  • legal or regulatory authorities when disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety or security; and
  • parties to a business transaction involving financing, acquisition, reorganization or sale, subject to appropriate safeguards.

7. International processing

PostaraTrend is based in Canada and may use service providers in Canada, the United States and other jurisdictions. Personal information may therefore be processed outside your province, state or country and may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions. Where applicable law requires specific transfer safeguards, we use appropriate contractual or other recognized mechanisms.

8. Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, to provide the service, meet contractual or legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security and enforce agreements. Retention varies by data type and context.

  • Active account and workspace data is generally retained while the account or workspace is in use.
  • Billing, tax and transaction records may be retained for periods required by law.
  • Security, audit and system logs may be retained for operational, fraud-prevention and legal purposes.
  • Backups may continue to contain deleted information for a limited period until they are overwritten or expire under backup schedules.
  • Aggregated or de-identified information that no longer reasonably identifies a person may be retained for analysis and service improvement.

See our Data Deletion Policy for request procedures.

9. Cookies and analytics

Our websites and applications may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies for authentication, security, preferences, analytics and product functionality. You can control cookies through your browser. Disabling required technologies may prevent some features from working.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability or restriction of personal information, to object to certain uses, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

If your information is held in an employer or agency workspace, that organization may be responsible for responding to your request. We may direct your request to that organization or assist it in responding.

To make a request, email inquiries@postaratrend.ca. We may need to verify your identity and will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

11. Security

We use administrative, technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or loss. No system can be guaranteed completely secure. Users are responsible for protecting their credentials and for using appropriate access controls within organization workspaces.

12. Children

Our products are not directed to children under 13. Where a product or organization account is used in an educational or youth context, the responsible organization must have any consent or legal authority required to provide the information to us.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as our products, vendors or legal obligations change. We will update the "Last updated" date and, where required, provide additional notice of material changes.
