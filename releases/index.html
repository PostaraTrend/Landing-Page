FAIR QUESTIONS

What buyers ask us before they sign.

What will the system refuse to publish?

Death and tragedy exploitation, active partisan controversy, claims it cannot ground in a real source, and anything that falls outside the rules calibrated for your business. When the call is close, it suppresses and records the reason instead of gambling with your name.

What happens when it gets something wrong?

Every decision is logged with its reason, published and refused alike, so a miss is visible instead of buried. When a rule needs tightening, we tighten it in code, and the change applies to every post from that moment on. You are never relying on a promise to be more careful.

Who owns the accounts and the content?

You do. The system publishes to pages you own, and everything it publishes or declines on your behalf is delivered to you in the monthly evidence report. If we part ways, your pages, your content, and your record go with you.

How do we start?

With a conversation about what you sell, where you sell it, and what must never be said. We calibrate your rules first, then the system begins publishing on your pages with every decision on the record from day one. Use the contact form and tell us about your business.

We publish our own numbers.

The homepage counters count everything our fleet has published and refused since June 18, 2026, refreshed from the live logs. The refusals are on the record because the judgement is the product.
