Role Scout at a glance

Role Scout takes you from a resume to an interview room without ever saying something about you that is not true. Every score, tailored resume, cover letter and interview answer is built only from lines you have confirmed as true.

The journey in one line

Set preferences (country, target roles) → upload your resume (PDF, Word or LinkedIn export) → confirm every line and attest → matches arrive scored with reasons → tailor and apply → track the application → prepare for the interview. First run takes about fifteen minutes; after that everything is minutes.

What it does

Free resume check

Before signing up, anyone can see whether their resume can be read. Nothing is stored.

Honest matching

Real postings scored 0 to 100 against your attested lines, always with a plain reason. Canadian postings are tagged with their NOC code, TEER level and an Express Entry level chip where it applies; new postings are tagged nightly.

Verified tailoring

A resume rewritten for one posting, every line traced to your attested lines and checked by an independent verifier. Changes are marked: reworded, merged, title mapped. When it cannot verify, it refuses and says why.

Cover letters

Written from your attested resume for the posting, checked claim by claim. Download as PDF or Word.

Bring any posting

Paste a posting you found anywhere; it is scored within a minute and can be tailored like any match.

Verification links

Share a private link showing an employer your verified resume with its checks. You see every open, and you can rotate or turn the link off at any time.

Applications board

Applied, Interview, Offer, Hired, Closed. Moving a card to Interview builds an interview pack automatically.

Interview preparation

Likely questions, talking points with evidence, honest gaps with what you can truthfully say, practice with feedback, and timed mock interviews.

30 60 90 day work plan

A plan for the exact role built from your attested resume: what you would do in the first 30, 60 and 90 days, each item backed by an evidence line. Download it and bring it to the interview.

Daily digest

One email at 08:30 on days when new roles score 60 or above for you. Nothing on quiet days. Off in one switch.

Versions and Active version

Every upload is a version with its own attestation. You choose which attested version drives your account, useful when you keep two true resumes.

Works as a phone app

Installs from the browser with no app store, stays readable offline, and always tells you honestly when it is waiting or when a connection is needed.

Peer confirmation

Ask a former manager or colleague to confirm a line is true. Their confirmation travels with your verified resume.

What it will never do

  • Invent a skill, date, number or achievement your attested lines do not state.
  • Apply on your behalf or fill in an employer's form. Applying is always your own action.
  • Feed you answers during a live interview.
  • Show a score it cannot explain.
  • Store anything from the free check, or use your data to train AI models. Your data is stored in Canada.

Who it serves

Job seekers, including newcomers to Canada

A resume employers can trust, in Canadian language, with your original titles preserved and your gaps handled honestly.

Recruiters and counsellors

A candidate's verification link proves every line was attested, shows every change, and needs no account to read. Organization workspaces are planned; tell us what you would pay for first.
