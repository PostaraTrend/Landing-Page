Step 1 of 5: Upload and transcription
How it works

Six steps. Nothing invented at any of them.

1Upload

Your resume is transcribed line by line, verbatim. Nothing is changed and nothing is added.

2Attest

You review every line and confirm it is true. Everything downstream rests on your word.

3Match

Real postings in your country are scored against your attested lines. Gaps are named plainly, never hidden.

4Tailor

A tailored resume and cover letter are drafted where every line traces to attested content. Anything unsupported is refused and logged.

5Verify and share

An independent check confirms provenance. You can share a live verification link an employer can open, and see when they do.

6Prepare and practise

When you reach an interview, a preparation pack is built for that exact role, honest about the gaps. Practise your answers and every sentence is checked against what you attested. Then a 30 60 90 day plan for the role, ready to present and edit.

The verified banner

What verification means, exactly.

When a tailored resume carries the verified banner, an independent check has confirmed that every line of it is supported by the lines you attested, and that nothing was invented by the system. The verification page shows an employer the provenance counts behind that claim.

Verification is a statement about provenance, not a guarantee of your career history. That part is yours, which is the point: Role Scout puts your word back at the center and then protects it. When the system cannot support a line from your attested content, it refuses to write the line, and the refusal is recorded.

Fair questions

Asked plainly, answered plainly.

Will it make my resume look better than I am?

No, and that is deliberate. Role Scout reorders, selects, and phrases from what you attested. It will not add a skill, a title, or a number you did not claim. When a posting asks for something your attested resume does not contain, the gap is shown to you and nothing is written to cover it.

What does the employer actually see?

Only what you choose to share: a tailored resume, its verified status, and the provenance counts. Your attested source resume is never shown on a verification page, and sharing is off by default.

Where do the job postings come from?

From public employer job boards, refreshed automatically, with every posting linking back to its source. Postings that leave their source are retired.
