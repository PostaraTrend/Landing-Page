The resume pile has stopped working, for everyone

Employers are fighting a flood of AI written applications, most saying whatever the posting wants to hear. Ghosting is common on both sides. Job seekers respond by inflating claims to compete, which makes every resume harder to believe, including the honest ones.

  • An employer reading your resume has no way to tell what is true and what is optimized.
  • A generic AI resume builder makes this worse, not better, since it can write a confident claim for anything.
  • Nobody wins a race to sound the most impressive. Somebody has to be verifiably believable instead.

No invented statistics here by design. These are widely reported, qualitative trends, not numbers we made up to sound impressive.

For job seekers: five steps, from upload to a resume an employer can check

This is the whole process. Nothing happens to your resume that you have not confirmed yourself.

1

Upload

Bring your resume as it is. Nothing is rewritten yet.

2

Attest

Confirm, line by line, that each claim is true. This is the foundation everything else stands on.

3

Match

TraceScout finds real, currently open roles from employer career pages and public job boards.

4

Tailor

A version is built for a specific posting, using only what you attested to. Nothing invented, ever.

5

Verify

A shareable link shows the employer exactly which lines are backed, with a banner they can trust.

Why this is not something a competitor can copy overnight

Most resume tools compete on how confidently they can write a sentence. TraceScout competes on whether the sentence is true, and shows its work.

Attestation

Every claim is confirmed by you before it is ever used, not assumed true by a parser reading a PDF.

Line provenance

Each line of a tailored resume traces back to an attested source. An employer can see exactly where a claim came from.

The refusal ledger

When there is no attested basis for a claim a posting wants, TraceScout refuses to invent one, and records the refusal rather than hiding it.

Independent verification

A link an employer can open on their own, outside your control, showing the same honest picture every time.

Fair questions

Does TraceScout verify that my career facts are true?

No. Verification here means provenance, showing that a claim traces back to something you attested, not an independent check on whether the underlying fact itself is true. You own the truth of your own attestation.

What happens if a posting wants something I have not done?

TraceScout tells you honestly, and will not invent experience to close the gap. The refusal is recorded rather than papered over.

Is my resume shared with anyone without my say?

No. A verification link is only created and shared when you choose to share it, and you can disable it at any time.

Where is my resume stored?

In Canada. Data residency stays true regardless of which country you are searching in.
